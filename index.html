The AYAT Story

Interiors designed to be read, room by room

Founded in Mumbai, AYAT Interior has spent over a decade shaping homes, workplaces and hospitality spaces that hold together in feeling and function. We work as designer and contractor in one — a single studio accountable for the idea and the execution.

From the first sketch to the final handover, every material, joint and finish is chosen with intent. It's less about following trends and more about giving each client's space a voice of its own.

Our Process

Five Verses, One Finished Space

Ayah 01

Discover & Brief

We spend time understanding how you'll use the space — your habits, your collection, your budget — before a single line is drawn.

Ayah 02

Concept & Design

Moodboards, 3D visualisations and material palettes are refined with you until the concept feels unmistakably yours.

Ayah 03

Detailing & Procurement

Every joinery drawing, finish schedule and fixture is specified and sourced from vetted vendors and our in-house workshop.

Ayah 04

Execution On Site

Our engineers and skilled workforce manage civil work, carpentry, electricals and finishing under one accountable roof.

Ayah 05

Handover & Aftercare

A styled, move-in ready space with a defect-liability period and support long after the final walkthrough.
